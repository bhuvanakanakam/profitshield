A QUICK TOUR

A clear path from records to a decision.

01 · Financial workspace

Load the synthetic example or import normalized CSVs. Net sales and contribution margin use your imported records. Missing costs stay unavailable.

02 · Findings & explanations

Open the evidence behind a finding. Ask Guru for a rule-based explanation using the same baseline. Suspected exposure is not recovered money.

03 · Scenarios & approval

Compare price, cost and commission assumptions. Propose a scenario, then type the exact phrase to record a simulated action. This workspace never changes a platform menu.

04 · Dispute drafts

Prepare and download a draft with the supporting records. Review and submit it yourself. Duplicate invoice copies produce inquiries, not payment claims.

05 · Historical books & Connections

Explore the original sample orders and recipe-based estimates. Imports and reviewed receipts here update historical books, a separate dataset from the Financial workspace.

06 · Your session

Your owner password protects this app. Sign out when finished. Square integrations are sandbox-only; external AI is optional and needs privacy approval.
